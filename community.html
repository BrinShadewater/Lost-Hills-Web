I've lived here twelve years and I can tell you that nobody really gets a straight answer on the Continuity Grid. The council voted to approve it back in ’91, I think. The vote was unanimous, which is unusual because Councilman Redd never agrees with Councilwoman Park on anything.

Hi, L. Tashiro from Megabyte. We've seen this with a couple of USRobotics units on the CityNet access number. There's a handshake bug in firmware revision 3.4 that can trigger a spontaneous dial cycle when the line is idle for more than six hours. Regarding the phone line being plugged in — I'd suggest checking if a family member or someone with a spare key might have reconnected it, or whether the plug wasn't fully out. The firmware bug doesn't allow the modem to physically reconnect a cable.
