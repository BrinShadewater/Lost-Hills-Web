[#]Welcome to Lost Hills, Washington

Lost Hills Online is the official municipal information network of the City of Lost Hills. Launched in May 1993 as a civic technology pilot in partnership with Shadewater Labs, Megabyte Computers, Northwestern Interstate Bank, and the Lost Hills Chamber of Commerce, CityNet provides residents and visitors with public records, civic notices, business listings, and community resources.

What Happened on May 21–22, 1993

The Shadewater Applied Systems Conference was scheduled to conclude on May 21 with a final integrated demonstration combining experimental systems (Horizon Mapping, Lattice Relay, Corridor Imaging). The City of Lost Hills municipal archive and its connected CityNet terminals documented this event.

At 03:17:04 PDT on May 22—approximately 18 hours after the final day opened—the archive was placed in emergency standby by EACS (Emergency Archive Continuity System). The cause is not documented.

Concurrent anomalies reported that evening:

  • Sezzler Hotel: 41 incoming calls logged between 03:17 and 03:18 PDT (building capacity: 12 lines)
  • Downtown Lost Hills: Clock synchronization failures reported; timepieces stopping or running slow
  • Catfish Lake: Environmental monitoring array recorded anomalous readings; survey depth exceeds instrument range
  • CityNet System: All terminals and archive frozen. Field contact re-established months or years later from unknown location
