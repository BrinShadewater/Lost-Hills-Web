[#]City Clerk's Office

The City Clerk's Office is the records authority for the City of Lost Hills. The Clerk maintains civic notices, archive corrections, public-records requests, meeting minutes, and the CommunityNet moderation log. The Clerk has held office since 1985.

About Public Records

Civic records are open to the public under Washington State law. Records withheld pending review remain subject to release once verification is complete. The Clerk's Office does not retain records on behalf of private entities, including Shadewater Laboratories.
