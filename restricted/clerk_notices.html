Internal Memo File — D. Wakeman, City Clerk

These are my working notes regarding the CityNet archive anomalies beginning May 17, 1993. I am keeping them here because my paper copies were returned to me in a different order than I filed them. I did not rearrange them.

MEMO — 05/17/1993 Conference opens today. CityNet traffic is high. The Shadewater content team has filed a request to edit several pages directly, bypassing my review queue. I denied the request. L. Tashiro tells me this is normal for the conference platform. I told him it is not normal for the city archive. We are keeping the review queue.
MEMO — 05/18/1993 The Horizon Mapping System session listing on conference.html shows Pavilion B12 as the venue. Sezzler management states there is no Pavilion B12. I have asked Shadewater to correct the entry. Shadewater has not responded. Entry remains on the page.
MEMO — 05/19/1993 (morning) Someone has inserted a maintenance notice on sezzler.html and an access restriction notice on catfish.html. Neither was in my queue. The Sezzler notice is signed as a Shadewater EACS operation. I did not authorize EACS to write to city pages. When I asked Shadewater to explain EACS edit access, I was told it was a "standard continuity feature." I am asking for this in writing. I have not received a written response.
MEMO — 05/19/1993 (afternoon) The staff directory on shadewater.html has been removed. The page now says it was removed at 03:17 by the EACS process. I did not receive a notice. There is no one in the Shadewater communications office to call. I went to the campus in person; the visitor center was closed. The gate guard told me the campus was in a pre-conference restricted configuration. I pointed out the conference started on Monday and it is now Thursday. He agreed this was unusual.
MEMO — 05/20/1993 Seven pages now show unauthorized edits from the EACS process. I have flagged each one with a review notice. Three of the review notices were removed overnight. I re-added them. They were removed again by morning. The removal timestamps all read 03:17.

I have asked Public Works to review the CityNet server access log. They report that citynet03 shows no record of edit sessions at 03:17. The session log for that period shows all fields as null. This is not a normal server state.
MEMO — 05/21/1993 (before 02:00) The civic notice for today has been placed in my queue by a system account I don't recognize. Account name: EACS_CONTINUITY_1. The notice describes the activation of the Continuity Grid in a way that was not in my pre-conference briefing. I am declining to publish it until I can reach someone at Shadewater or Public Works. It is 1:44 AM. I cannot reach anyone.

The notice says residents should expect "brief power, telephone, and clock interruptions between 02:00 and 03:30." I am looking out my office window. The Shadewater building lights are on.
MEMO — 05/21/1993 (after 03:17) I am writing this down while I remember it clearly. My office clock stopped at 03:17 and restarted at 03:17. The power went off once. When it came back, the archive terminal showed a new session in progress. I don't know from where.

Fourteen pages have new edits. Some pages have content I don't recognize that was not in any draft. The night_entry form on community.html has posts from accounts that don't exist. One post says it was filed at 03:17. It is 245 words long. I read it once. I am not going to read it again.

I am going to flag everything I can tonight and come back in the morning to write a formal report to the mayor. This is bigger than a server maintenance issue.
MEMO — 05/22/1993 I filed the report with the mayor's office this morning. I have flagged 23 pages for review. I have formally requested citynet03 be taken offline pending an audit.

R. Olesen at Public Works says the server is not responding to a shutdown command. It is not on the decommission schedule. He says it cannot be reached for shutdown. I asked what that means. He said it means what it means.

One person is still using the archive. I can see them in the access log. The session originated from a terminal at Catfish Lake south pier. There is no terminal at Catfish Lake south pier. I told R. Olesen. He wrote it down.
MEMO — 05/22/1993 (evening) I went to the south pier this afternoon. There was no terminal. There was a power cable coiled neatly at the end of the pier. It was connected to nothing on either end. I left it there. I don't know why I left it there.

I have requested the clerk_removed_articles page be taken down. It was removed this morning. It is back now. The removal timestamp says 03:17. It is 6:30 PM.
NOTE (undated) I don't know what the archive thinks it's preserving. I don't know what it thinks happened. I do know that the city of Lost Hills was a normal city on May 16 and I am not sure what it is now.

If you are reading this: the archive is not reliable. I am still here. You can reach me at the office or by the number on the contact page. The contact page is probably still right. I haven't been able to change it.

— D. Wakeman, Clerk
